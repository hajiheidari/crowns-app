# Changelog

Each entry is also shown in the in-app "What's new" sheet.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-09-28
- New Stats screen: current and best streak, a calendar of solved dailies, and best, average and recent times for each difficulty.
- New Settings: auto-✕, tap order, drag to mark, conflict highlight, timer, haptics and light or dark theme.
- Optional daily reminder at a time you choose. We only ask after you solve your first daily puzzle.
- This "What's new" sheet now appears once after each update.

## 0.3.1 — 2026-09-28
- The tutorial no longer opens by itself. Tap "How to play" at the bottom of the home screen whenever you want it.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-09-28
- New animated tutorial on first launch shows the rules and controls in under a minute. Open it again any time with "How to play".
- New Hint button: it shows where to look, explains why in plain words, and can make the move for you. Wrong marks are pointed out first.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-09-28
- Play today's daily puzzle and Unlimited puzzles in Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert.
- Tap to cycle ✕ / crown / empty, long-press for a crown, drag to paint ✕.
- Auto-✕, red highlight for clashing crowns, unlimited undo / redo, clear, timer and pause.
- Progress saves on every move and resumes after the app is closed. Daily streak.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-28
- Project set up: Expo app with a blank Home screen, puzzle engine and 2,696 bundled puzzles moved in.
